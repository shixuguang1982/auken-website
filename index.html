About Auken

Your Trusted Machinery Partner from China

Zhengzhou Auken Machinery Equipment Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer specializing in construction machinery, wood processing equipment and metal recycling machines. Located in Zhengzhou — China's major industrial hub — we combine advanced manufacturing with deep export experience.

Our products serve customers across Africa, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and South America. We understand the unique demands of emerging markets and deliver machinery that balances performance, durability and affordability.
